Why it's limited

We onboard in waves —
on purpose.

teemeup is in a focused industry preview, working closely with golfers and insiders to refine the experience before public launch. Inviting everyone at once would dilute that. Invitations are limited, and founding spots fill before the waitlist opens — so the earlier you join, the sooner you play.
