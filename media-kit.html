05 — Brand guidelines

Voice & usage rules

Brand voice

  • Confident, not arrogant
  • Knowledgeable — like a seasoned caddie
  • Conversational and direct
  • Golfer-first, technology second

Naming rules

  • Always teemeup — lowercase, one word
  • The AI agent is Bob, not "the bot"
  • Write teemeup.ai, never "Teemeup"
  • Parent company: Courserev.ai

Please avoid

  • Recolouring or distorting the logo
  • Placing logo on clashing backgrounds
  • Claiming partnership without written approval
  • Altering or cropping app screenshots
