What is teemeup?

teemeup is your personal AI golf assistant — designed to take the friction out of planning and booking a round of golf. Instead of jumping between apps, calling the pro shop, or filling out forms, you simply have a conversation with Bob, our AI caddie, and he handles everything for you.

Tell Bob where you want to play, when, and what conditions you're looking for. He finds the best available tee times, checks the weather and course conditions, and completes the booking — all in a single conversation, through the channels you already use.

How Bob works

Bob is a conversational AI built specifically for golf. You can speak to him naturally — no commands, no keywords. He understands context, remembers your preferences, and gets better at recommending courses and tee times the more you use him.

  • Say or type what you want: "dry 18-hole near Edinburgh this Saturday under £60"
  • Bob searches real-time availability from courses connected through our partner network.
  • He surfaces the best options ranked by weather, distance, price, and your history — not alphabetically.
  • Confirm with a single message. Booking, calendar invite and directions are sent automatically.

Channels & platforms

teemeup works wherever you already are. Bob is available across:

  • The teemeup app — native mobile experience with voice support
  • WhatsApp — chat with Bob from your existing WhatsApp account
  • SMS / iMessage — no app required, works on any phone
  • ChatGPT — via the teemeup plugin for ChatGPT Plus users

Your preferences and booking history sync automatically across all channels.

Course coverage

teemeup connects to golf courses through Courserev.ai, our booking infrastructure partner. We currently serve courses across the UK, Ireland, and select markets in the US and Europe, with coverage expanding rapidly as new courses join our partner network.

If your club isn't listed yet, you can submit a request through the app or email us at hello@teemeup.ai and we'll prioritise it.
