# teemeup.ai

> teemeup is an AI-powered golf booking platform. Bob, the AI golf agent, helps golfers book tee times, check weather, coordinate group rounds, and manage their game — entirely through natural conversation on WhatsApp, SMS, iMessage, ChatGPT, or the teemeup app.

## What teemeup does

teemeup lets golfers book tee times at golf courses through a conversational AI agent called Bob. Instead of browsing multiple booking sites, a golfer types or speaks naturally — "dry 18-hole near Charlotte this Saturday under $60" — and Bob finds options, ranks them by weather, distance, and price, and confirms the booking without the golfer leaving the chat.

Bob is available on WhatsApp, SMS, iMessage, ChatGPT (native integration), and the teemeup web and mobile app. No app download required to start.

## Key capabilities

- Tee time search and booking via real-time course availability (powered by Courserev.ai)
- Voice booking: tap the mic, describe what you want, Bob handles the rest
- Weather-smart recommendations: every suggestion includes wind, rain probability, and a playability score
- Group booking coordination: finds a slot that works for multiple players
- Round manager: confirmation numbers, tee time reminders, course directions, calendar invites
- Personalised recommendations based on handicap, home club, budget, and weather preferences
- Post-booking management: reschedule or cancel via chat, no phone calls needed

## Who it is for

- Golfers who want to book tee times without navigating multiple booking platforms
- Golf groups coordinating shared tee times
- Golfers who prefer voice or messaging interfaces over apps and forms
- Golf courses and venues that want an automated AI booking channel (via Courserev.ai partner integration)
- Brands and partners looking to reach golfers at the moment of booking intent

## Current status

teemeup is in invite-only industry preview as of June 2026. Coverage spans courses across the UK, US, Europe, Asia, and beyond via major booking networks. A developer SDK for embedding golf booking is coming soon.

## Company

teemeup.ai is operated by the team behind Courserev.ai, the B2B Voice AI platform for golf courses. The product was introduced publicly at Golfweek Tech Lab at the 2026 PGA Championship.
