Our story

Built by golfers,
for every golfer

We got tired of juggling course websites, phone calls and half-working booking apps just to arrange a round. So we built the thing we always wanted — an AI caddie who handles all of it with a single message.

Our mission

Make booking a tee time as simple as texting a friend — no tab-hopping, no forms to fill, no phone queues. Just ask Bob.

How it started

The round that needed a better booking experience

It started with a simple frustration. Trying to organise a Saturday morning four-ball meant fifteen minutes of tab-hopping, two phone calls to courses that rang out, and a group chat full of "is that still happening?" messages.

Golf is a social game. The booking process shouldn't get in the way of that. We knew conversational AI had reached the point where a genuinely helpful agent was possible — one that understood golf the way a seasoned caddie does.

We built Bob: a golf-native AI agent that lives wherever you already message. Tell him what you want — when, where, how many players, what budget — and he handles the rest. Course search, weather check, tee time availability, booking confirmation, reminders. Done.

teemeup is built by the team behind Courserev.ai, giving us deep roots in how golf courses operate, what booking partners need, and what golfers actually care about.

What we believe

How we build

Four principles that guide every product decision we make.

Golfer-first, always

Every feature starts with a real golfer frustration. If it doesn't make booking faster, clearer or more enjoyable, it doesn't ship.

AI that earns trust

Bob never books without asking first. He shows his reasoning, surfaces real options, and lets you stay in control. Helpful, not presumptuous.

Works where you already are

Bob lives on WhatsApp, SMS, iMessage and ChatGPT — plus the dedicated teemeup app. Wherever you message, Bob is there.

Reliability over hype

We'd rather ship fewer features that actually work than demo impressive things that let golfers down on the first tee.
